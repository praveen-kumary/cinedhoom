3. Formal DMCA Takedown Notice Requirements

If you are a copyright owner, or an authorized agent thereof, and believe that any content, link, or preview indexed on CineDhoom infringes upon your copyright, you may submit a formal notification pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) providing the following statutory information:

  • Physical or Electronic Signature: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  • Identification of the Copyrighted Work: Clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (e.g., official movie title, production studio, copyright registration number).

  • Perjury Statement: A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information provided in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Anti-Piracy Policy & Lawful Streaming Advocacy

Our Commitment to Content Creators
STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE ANTI-PIRACY PLEDGE:

CineDhoom is 100% committed to supporting the theatrical film industry and legitimate OTT streaming ecosystems. We strictly condemn digital piracy, cam-rips, illegal torrent networks, and unauthorized stream re-broadcasting.

1. The Reality of Cinema & Film Economics

Every movie is the culmination of years of relentless labor by thousands of creative artists, directors, technicians, lightmen, musicians, and stunt coordinators. Film production requires immense financial capital and artistic dedication. Digital copyright piracy inflicts devastating economic harm upon creative workers and production ecosystems across South Indian, Bollywood, and international cinema.

2. How CineDhoom Directs Audiences to Lawful Platforms

Our mission is to help moviegoers discover legitimate, authorized avenues to enjoy cinema. Whenever a movie is in theaters, we encourage viewers to purchase official tickets via platforms like BookMyShow, Paytm Movies, or local box office theaters. Whenever a film transitions to digital distribution, we provide direct references to official licensed OTT subscriptions (Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, Aha, ZEE5, SonyLIV).

3. Prohibition of Pirated Media Links

Users and visitors are expressly advised that:

  • CineDhoom does NOT provide torrent magnet links, direct download links, or unauthorized cyberlocker links.
  • CineDhoom does NOT bypass digital rights management (DRM) systems or encourage copyright infringement.
  • Any comments or user submissions containing links to pirated domains (e.g. torrent sites, unauthorized stream hosts) are automatically scrubbed and deleted.

4. Direct Industry Collaboration

We welcome proactive collaboration with film production houses, producers' councils, and anti-piracy cells across India and globally. Inquiries, partnership requests, or compliance communications can be directed to: contact@cinedhoom.com.
